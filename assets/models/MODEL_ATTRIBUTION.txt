--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ATTRIBUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Murya — Multi-Speaker Offline-Capable Hausa Neural Speech Synthesis
  © 2026 Adamu Danjuma Abubakar · https://adamu.tech

  Murya is an 8-speaker (4 male, 4 female) Piper/VITS model exported to ONNX for
  CPU-only, offline inference. Acoustic tuning targets Standard Kano Hausa
  (Eastern dialect). This application uses the model unmodified apart from
  ONNX Runtime dynamic INT8 quantisation performed for low-end Android devices;
  the quantised graph is distributed under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.

TRAINING DATA ATTRIBUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Fine-tuned on the WAXAL Hausa TTS subset (CC BY 4.0):

    Diack, A., Nelson, P., Agbesi, K., et al.
    WAXAL: A Large-Scale Multilingual African Language Speech Corpus.
    arXiv:2602.02734, 2026.
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/WaxalNLP

  WAXAL is used as fine-tuning data only; the model architecture, inference
  pipeline, tonal handling and deployment stack are original work.
